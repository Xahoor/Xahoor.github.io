Membership

Become a member of the Swat Women Rise Initiative and be part of a community dedicated to empowerment.

Anyone who shares our vision and is committed to supporting women's empowerment in Swat is eligible to apply.

Members get exclusive updates, invitations to our events, and the opportunity to vote on key organizational decisions.

Fill out the membership form, attend an orientation session, and contribute the annual membership fee.

FAQ

Who can volunteer?

Anyone with a passion for our cause and some time to spare.

How can I become a member?

By filling out the application form and meeting the eligibility criteria.

Can men participate?

Yes, we believe in co-education and community engagement involving everyone.

How can schools partner?

Schools can partner by sharing resources or collaborating on educational programs.

Mission Statement

Educating girls, empowering women, and building stronger communities through education, leadership, and collaboration.
